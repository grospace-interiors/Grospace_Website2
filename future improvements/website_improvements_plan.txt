# Website Improvement Plan

This plan is based on the feedback and analysis provided for the Grospace website. We will address these points one by one, starting with the "MUST-HAVE" items.

## MUST-HAVE

1.  **Implement WhatsApp chat feature.**
2.  **Implement a call button.**
3.  **Implement a "Book Free Site Visit" form.**
4.  **Implement a testimonials section.**
5.  **Add real execution photos.**
6.  **Explain the process (how work happens).**
7.  **Ensure easy navigation and page structure (review "Projects", "Contact", "About" accessibility).**
8.  **Create or enhance a contact page/section.**
9.  **Add a floating WhatsApp button.**
10. **Add click-to-call functionality on mobile.**

## IMPORTANT

11. **Implement simple project pages or galleries.**
12. **Optimize site performance (loading speed, images, videos).**
