# Grospace Interiors — Website Structure, Routes & Content Plan

## First Impression Review

Your website already has:

* Premium modern look
* Good luxury interior aesthetic
* Strong black + white + soft red branding
* Clean package flow
* Nice calculator concept
* Good CTA placements

But currently it still feels:

* Incomplete in content depth
* Slightly template-like in some sections
* Missing trust-building storytelling
* Weak route hierarchy
* Missing SEO structure
* Some pages feel disconnected
* Not enough emotional copywriting

The biggest opportunity:
You are not just selling furniture.
You are selling:

* Stress-free home setup
* Complete home transformation
* Budget clarity
* Modular customization
* Trust
* Execution support

The website should feel like:
"A modern interior company that makes furnishing simple for middle + upper-middle-class families in Bhopal."
